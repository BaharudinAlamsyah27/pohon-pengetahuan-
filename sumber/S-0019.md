## Teks Asli
neuroscience shows that the easiest way to become confident and actually believe in yourself is to change your self-talk. 

athletes do it, authors do it, artists do it. anyone successful or performing at a high level has used the power of self-talk to get themselves there.

so here's exactly how you're gonna do it: 

1. no negative self-talk: you're gonna stop saying negative things about yourself, out loud or in your head, and no, i don't care if you're joking or not. even if it's a joke, your brain believes everything that you tell it. it does not know the difference between what's fake, what's a joke, and what's reality, because your subconscious mind is always listening to what you feed it, and it will start to identify with whatever you tell it.

2. reframe your struggles: instead of saying "i am struggling," tell yourself "i am learning." instead of saying "this is so hard," tell yourself "this is getting easier." 

if you put a negative label on your experiences, then that's what you're gonna experience. the more that you have the mindset of "i am learning, this is easy," the easier and the more fun you're gonna have getting to where you need to be.

3. speak to yourself like you are someone you love: because you are, you should be. you are in your own body and with yourself for the rest of your life, so compassionate and kind communication to yourself is so important, and it makes up like 95% of your confidence.

it's also important to remember that when you're speaking to yourself, you're not only speaking to yourself now, you're speaking to your inner child. so the next time you say something negative to yourself, literally imagine a younger version of you, a photo of little you in your mind, and remind yourself that you're speaking to that little one. and it will transform everything.
