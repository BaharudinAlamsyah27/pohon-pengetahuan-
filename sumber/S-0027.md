## Teks Asli
Hate to say this, but being a generic software engineer is becoming a terrible career strategy.

I see two ways to stay relevant:

Pair software engineering with another domain: business, finance, marketing, etc. Become someone who understands both technology and the business.

Go extremely deep into technology: Linux kernels, distributed systems, inference engineering, databases, infrastructure, etc.

Simply knowing how to build REST APIs and React applications won't be enough anymore.

The era of being a generic software engineer is coming to an end. Specialisation is becoming a necessity.
