## Teks Asli
science shows that complaining or talking about your problems the wrong way can actually make your brain and your anxiety worse, and i will tell you the neuroscience behind why and what to do instead.

most people think they're just talking about their problems to get it off their chest, and it's fine, but research in clinical psychology shows that repeatedly talking about your problems without finding a resolution can prolong negative emotions and increase stress.

and brain imaging studies show that talking about emotional pain without reframing it keeps your amygdala, which is involved in stress and anxiety, active.

but what's even more important is that the more you complain about your problems, the more you train your brain to look for more problems, because your brain filters your reality based on what you focus on. and this will keep you from ever coming to a solution because you can't solve a problem with the same thinking that created it.

you might be thinking, "oh, but talking about my problems helps me feel closer to someone." and while yes, it can do that, research also shows that it can increase anxiety and depression in both people. and i'm sure you've noticed this. that friend who always calls you to talk about what's wrong? you often feel drained afterward.

but there's a right way to do this, because venting itself isn't the problem. it's how you do it. 

talking about your problems can be regulating if it leaves you feeling calm, clear and connected afterward.

so next time you complain or talk about your problems, ask yourself, do i feel closer to moving forward? if not, you're not processing the problem. you are practicing the problem.
