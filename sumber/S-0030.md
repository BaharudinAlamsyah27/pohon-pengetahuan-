## Teks Asli
If you're under 30 and broke, I'm begging you:

Learn AI inference engineering.

> Pick one area: GPU programming, CUDA, model optimization, quantization or distributed inference.
> Lock yourself in and master the fundamentals of how models actually run.
> Learn PyTorch, Triton, vLLM, TensorRT-LLM and inference serving.
> Build 3–5 projects: optimize an LLM, reduce inference costs, improve latency or build a high-throughput serving system.
> Benchmark everything. Learn to squeeze more tokens out of every GPU.
> Make a list of 50 AI startups, GPU cloud providers and inference companies you'd love to work for.
> Study their infrastructure bottlenecks and build something that solves one.
> Reach out directly to founders and engineering leads. Show them your work and ask to intern.
> Work your ass off for 12–18 months. Learn how production AI infrastructure actually works.
> Then launch your own inference startup, optimization consultancy or GPU infrastructure company.

The AI boom isn't just about building smarter models.

It's about making intelligence cheaper, faster and accessible at scale.

The people who figure out how to serve intelligence efficiently could capture an enormous part of the AI economy.
