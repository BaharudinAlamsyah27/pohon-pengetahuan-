## Saripati
(1) Batasi penggunaan AI pada hal-hal mendasar agar kemampuan berpikir mandiri, pemahaman mendalam, dan keotentikan diri tidak tumpul.

## Teks Asli
things i stopped using AI for:

- understanding a research paper without reading it myself
- coming up with opinions on things
- writing personal messages to people
- making every small decision in my life
- learning a new concept without implementing it myself

the more i use AI, the more i realise how important it is to deliberately do certain things without it.
