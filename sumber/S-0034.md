## Teks Asli
neuroscience shows that your brain is constantly changing. the right daily habits can help you reshape your thinking, strengthen new patterns and become a different version of yourself.

start with these 6 habits:

1. visualize your future self every day: before bed, take 2 minutes to picture the person you're becoming.

how do they think? how do they act? what does their daily life look like?

your brain can activate some of the same neural networks during mental rehearsal as during actual experience. start rehearsing the person you want to become.

2. catch yourself playing small: notice when you hesitate, hold yourself back or convince yourself you're not capable of something.

write those moments down. these are clues to the old beliefs and patterns running in the background. you can't change a pattern you refuse to recognize.

3. rewrite one limiting belief every day: take a thought like "i'm not ready" and challenge it.

replace it with something more constructive: "i can learn as i go."
repeat it. act on it. give your brain new experiences that support the belief. repetition matters, but action is what makes the new belief more believable.

4. actually feel your gratitude: don't just say "i'm grateful for my family" or "i'm grateful for my home."

pause and think about WHY.

remember the feeling of safety, warmth and having people who care about you.
don't rush through it. give your attention to the good things already in your life.

5. give your brain proof that you're changing: want more confidence? speak up today. want more discipline? keep one small promise to yourself. want to become more capable? do something you've been avoiding.

your actions create experiences that can gradually reshape how you see yourself.

6. regulate your nervous system: your brain learns and adapts more effectively when you're not constantly overwhelmed by stress.

make room for calm every day. 

take a walk. practice slow breathing. spend a few minutes without your phone. give yourself time to reset.

you don't need to operate in survival mode all day.

here's what you need to understand: you're not stuck with the person you've been.

your beliefs aren't permanent. your habits aren't permanent. even the way you think about yourself can change.
your brain is adaptable.

but you have to give it something new to adapt to.

stop waiting to feel like a different person. start acting like one, one small decision at a time.
