## Saripati
Penjelasan aturan monetisasi konten asli (OCR) di X beserta bobot interaksi algoritma (mutual reply dan copy-link bernilai 20, sedangkan like bernilai 0,708).

## Teks Asli
how to start making more money on x

the play: quality geos & content > right niche > 3 original posts > replies that brings spotlight

> what actually pays

- original content rewards only counts premium home timeline impressions on original posts
- replies do not count toward the 500k threshold
- like weight is 0.708. reply is 5. mutual reply is 20. copy link share is 20

> quality country followers

- only engage accounts that are active in the last 30 days, post in english, and have follower/following ratio > 0.2
- reply under 5k - 40k accounts based in US, EU, SG, UAE. their mutuals are the people who pay for tools and services
- ignore pure airdrop lists iykyk
- check weekly: if new follows from US/EU/SG are under 15%, change the current posting angle for 14 days

> choosing the correct niche

- broad “web three alpha” or “ai tools” pulls farmers and dies
- the niches that print: free credits + agent setups that save real money, or ai, tech, finance
- us/eu builders search those exact phrases. they have premium. they dm for your services
- test for 14 days. keep only if 30%+ of new follows come from the paying geos

> number of posts per day

- 3 original posts max. 1 save post (how to + screenshot), 1 list, 1 personal/shitpost
- the algo scores your 2nd post in a viewer’s feed at 62.5% and the 3rd at ~44%. after that it collapses
- 15 - 20 quality replies on top. zero links in the main post (open link weight is 0.2)

> reply strategy

- reply to 8k - 35k accounts in your exact niche. add one number + one catch every time in the reply
- mid accounts where your audience is already there no single word replies
- follow back people who replied to you. mutuals get the 15 point boost
- don’t focus only on accounts with 100k+ followers, though you can still get good spotlight from them

> quality content that converts

- save posts get 4x the bookmark rate. bookmarks drive the dms and affiliate clicks
- lists get the reach. personal gets the follows. mix them 1:1:1
- show the result first (screenshot of costs, edge, or credits). then the steps after “show more”

> the catch

- premium home timeline impressions are the only thing x pays on. sponsorships come from the dms those posts generate
- a bad niche does not become profitable because you posted more
- none of this guarantees views. it only raises the odds of the impressions that actually pay

> what not to do

- don’t post 6+ originals to “stay consistent”
- don’t put links in the main post
- don’t stretch 3 useful lines into 30 to farm reading time

a hook gets someone to open the post. the rest needs to justify the time a reader spends on your post

run it for 14 days, and track the premium impression ratio
